Sunrise: Gentle Wake Alarm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Summary

Sunrise does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app uses stays on your iPhone, and we have no servers that receive information from the app.

This policy applies to the Sunrise: Gentle Wake Alarm app for iPhone, published by Michael Sheehy ("we", "us"). It explains what the app stores on your device, which permissions it asks for and why, and how purchases work.

Information stored on your device

To work, Sunrise keeps the following on your iPhone only. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

WhatWhy it's kept
Your alarms (times, repeat days, sounds, sunrise and light settings)To wake you as you've set up
App settings (night brightness, sleep goal, reminder timing, auto-start window)To remember your preferences
Wake history (when you stopped each alarm and how often you snoozed)To show your wake history; you can delete it in the app at any time
Your trial and purchase statusTo know whether the app is in its free trial or unlocked

The app's widgets, Control Center control and Live Activity read this information from a shared area on your iPhone so they can show your next alarm. It never leaves the device.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, this information is included in that backup under Apple's terms, as with any app. We have no access to your backups.

Permissions the app asks for

Sunrise asks for each permission only when it's needed and explains why first. You can change any of them later in your iPhone's Settings.

PermissionWhat it's used forWhat it's never used for
AlarmsScheduling your alarms so they ring even when your iPhone is locked or on silentAnything other than your alarms
NotificationsYour optional bedtime reminder and reminders about your free trialAdvertising or marketing
Camera (only if your iPhone requires it for the torch)Turning the torch on and off as part of your sunriseTaking photos or video; the camera is never used

Sunrise does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or any account details.

Purchases and the free trial

The 14-day free trial and the one-time "Unlock Sunrise" purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, email address, Apple Account details or payment information.

The app only receives confirmation from Apple that the trial has started or the purchase has been made, and checks this on your device. For how Apple handles purchase information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

Sunrise contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party software development kits. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not use your information for advertising.

If you use Siri, the Shortcuts app or automations with Sunrise (for example, to start bedside mode when you plug in), these run on your iPhone through Apple's own services. Any information they handle is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not sent to us.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics. These don't identify you and are used only to fix problems. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers.

Children

Sunrise is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we don't collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You remain in control of everything stored on your iPhone:

  • Delete your wake history in the app at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

If you're in the UK or EU, you still have your rights under data protection law, and you can contact us with any question about them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version of the app works differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
